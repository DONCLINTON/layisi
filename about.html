About Layisi

A marketplace that brings buyers and sellers together.

Layisi is an online marketplace built to help people across Uganda discover listings, promote what they offer and connect directly with potential buyers, customers, employers and service providers.

What you can find on Layisi

  • Products: browse and advertise new or used goods across categories such as phones, electronics, fashion, home and office, vehicles, agriculture and more.
  • Land and property: discover property listings and rental opportunities, or advertise a property.
  • Services: find and promote skills, professional services and local businesses.
  • Jobs: explore vacancies and allow employers or recruitment organisations to publish opportunities.
  • Buyer requests: describe what you need, state your budget and provide a way for interested sellers to contact you.

How Layisi works

Sellers and advertisers submit listing information through the platform. Visitors can browse listings and use the contact details or application information supplied in an advert to reach the person or organisation that posted it.

Layisi is a listing and connection platform. Buyers and sellers communicate and negotiate directly. Layisi does not collect or process the purchase price for transactions arranged between users, and does not arrange or guarantee delivery. Users independently agree on payment, collection or delivery, and any other transaction terms.

Trading safely

Review listing details carefully, ask questions, verify the identity and claims of the other party, and inspect goods or property where practical before paying. Do not send money or sensitive information to someone you have not adequately verified. Be especially cautious of job adverts that ask applicants to pay fees to obtain employment.

Our approach

We aim to make local discovery and direct connections easier through accessible listings and practical marketplace tools. Listings are supplied by users, so information should always be confirmed with the advertiser before making a decision.
