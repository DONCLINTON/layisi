Return and Refund Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

At Layisi, we want buyers and sellers to trade with confidence. Layisi connects people who want to buy and sell products, but transactions are currently completed directly between buyers and sellers. Layisi does not currently collect payments, process refunds, hold customer funds or manage product returns. Returns and refunds are therefore primarily handled through an agreement between the buyer and the seller, subject to applicable law.

1. How transactions work

When purchasing a product through Layisi:

  • Buyers communicate directly with sellers.
  • Buyers and sellers agree on the selling price and payment method.
  • Delivery or collection arrangements are agreed upon directly.
  • Any return, exchange or refund conditions should be discussed and agreed upon before payment.

We encourage buyers to clarify all important transaction details before committing to a purchase.

2. When can a product be returned?

A buyer may request a return when:

  • The product received is significantly different from its advertised description.
  • The product is damaged or defective upon delivery.
  • The seller supplied the wrong product.
  • The seller made a specific return or exchange commitment before the transaction.

Other return requests, including situations where a buyer simply changes their mind, will depend on the agreement between the buyer and seller and applicable consumer protection laws.

3. Agree on return conditions before buying

Buyers and sellers are encouraged to establish the following conditions before completing a transaction:

  • Whether returns are accepted.
  • The period within which a return can be requested.
  • Whether exchanges are permitted.
  • Who pays the cost of returning the product.
  • Whether refunds are available and how they will be issued.
  • The condition in which a returned product must be presented.

Where possible, keep these agreements in written communication for future reference.

4. Refunds

Since Layisi does not process payments, any approved refund is arranged directly between the buyer and seller.

The seller is responsible for fulfilling any refund commitment made during the transaction, subject to applicable law. Refund methods and timelines should be agreed upon directly by the parties.

5. Products that may have special return conditions

Some products may have special return conditions because of their nature, condition or intended use. Examples may include:

  • Perishable goods.
  • Personal hygiene products.
  • Custom-made or personalized products.
  • Digital products or services.
  • Products sold with clearly disclosed defects or damage.

These examples do not remove any mandatory consumer rights provided by applicable law.

6. Buyer responsibilities

Buyers are encouraged to:

  • Read product descriptions carefully.
  • Request additional photos or information where necessary.
  • Confirm the product's condition before payment.
  • Inspect products upon collection or delivery whenever possible.
  • Discuss return and refund conditions before making payment.
  • Keep evidence of payment and communications with the seller.

7. Seller responsibilities

Sellers using Layisi are expected to:

  • Provide accurate product descriptions and images.
  • Disclose known defects or damage.
  • Communicate honestly with prospective buyers.
  • Honour return, exchange and refund commitments made to buyers.
  • Respect applicable consumer protection requirements.

8. Layisi's role in disputes

Layisi provides the platform through which buyers and sellers discover products and communicate. We do not directly control transactions, payments, deliveries or refunds.

However, users may report misleading listings, suspected fraud, dishonest conduct or other marketplace-related concerns through the contact options available on our website.

Layisi may review reported listings and take appropriate action under its platform rules, including removing listings or restricting accounts where necessary. We cannot guarantee a refund or resolve every private transaction dispute.

9. Changes to this policy

Layisi may update this policy as the marketplace develops and introduces additional services. Any updated version will be published on this page.

10. Contact us

For questions about this policy, suspicious listings or marketplace-related concerns, please use the contact options available on the Layisi website.
