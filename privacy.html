Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Layisi handles information when you visit or use layisi.shop, browse listings, submit an advert or buyer request, or contact us.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Important: Layisi is an online marketplace that connects users. Buyers and sellers contact one another and arrange any transaction directly. Layisi does not process the purchase price or arrange delivery for those direct transactions. Information that you choose to include in a public listing may be visible to visitors.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

For this website, “Layisi”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the operator of layisi.shop. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at msngzclntn@gmail.com.

Layisi operates from Uganda. This website contact is provided for privacy enquiries. Where applicable law requires further controller details or an appointed representative, those details will be provided through an updated policy or directly in response to a request.

2. Information we may collect

The information depends on how you use the website. It may include:

  • Listing information: item or service descriptions, category, price, location, images, seller or business name, and contact details you submit for publication.
  • Buyer requests: requested item or service, budget, location, name, phone number and optional details.
  • Job adverts: job title, employer name, location, employment type, compensation details, description, contact number or application link.
  • Rental adverts: property type, neighbourhood, rent, advertiser type, contact name and phone number, description, landmarks and property images.
  • Messages to us: your email address and the information you include when contacting support.
  • Technical and usage information: information that may be supplied by your browser or service providers, such as IP address, device and browser information, pages viewed, approximate location derived from network information, timestamps and diagnostic events.
  • Local browser storage: Layisi may store preferences such as saved listings or saved searches on your device using browser storage. This helps the website remember your choices.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely necessary. Do not publish another person’s phone number, image or other personal information without a lawful reason and appropriate permission.

3. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • publish and display listings, job adverts, rental adverts and buyer requests;
  • allow visitors to contact the person or organisation that posted an advert;
  • operate, maintain, secure and improve the website and its features;
  • respond to support enquiries and investigate reports of misuse, fraud or unlawful content;
  • measure website traffic and understand how visitors use the service;
  • display and measure advertising, where advertising services are enabled;
  • meet legal obligations and protect the rights, property and safety of users and Layisi.

4. Public listings and contact details

Layisi is designed to help people connect directly. If you submit a listing, job advert, rental advert or buyer request, the information included in the published advert may be visible to the public, including your name, business name, location, phone number, images and description. Visitors may copy, save, share or contact you using that information.

Only submit contact details and other information that you are comfortable making public. If you need a listing corrected or removed, contact us. Removal from Layisi may not remove copies already saved, shared, indexed or cached by third parties.

5. Legal grounds for processing

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we rely on an appropriate legal basis for each processing purpose. Depending on the circumstances, this may include:

  • Contract or pre-contractual steps: to provide a feature you request, such as publishing and managing a listing.
  • Legitimate interests: to operate and secure the marketplace, prevent abuse, respond to enquiries and understand service performance, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Consent: where required, including for non-essential cookies or similar technologies and certain advertising or analytics activities.
  • Legal obligation: where processing is required to comply with applicable law.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before withdrawal.

6. Service providers and sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the website, including:

  • Supabase: the website code connects to Supabase services for database and file-storage functions. Information submitted through relevant forms may be stored or processed through those services.
  • Google Analytics: the website includes Google Analytics measurement code to help understand website usage.
  • Google advertising services: the website includes Google AdSense advertising code. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalise, limit or measure advertisements, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements.
  • Other technical providers: providers of website delivery, fonts, libraries, security, hosting or other functionality used by the website.

These providers may process information under their own privacy terms and instructions or roles under applicable law. We may also disclose information where required by law, to respond to lawful requests, or to protect users, the public or our rights. We do not sell personal information as a standalone data-broker product.

7. Cookies, local storage and advertising choices

Cookies and similar technologies may be used for essential website functions, security, remembering preferences, analytics and advertising. Layisi also uses browser local storage for features such as saved listings and saved searches; these are stored on your device.

Google Analytics and Google AdSense may set or read cookies or use similar identifiers, subject to their configuration and your choices. You can manage or delete browser cookies and site data through your browser settings. Blocking all storage may affect some website features.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: where required, non-essential cookies and personalised advertising technologies should only be used after valid consent through an appropriate consent-management mechanism. You should be able to change or withdraw your choices. The presence of this policy alone does not provide a consent mechanism. Layisi must ensure its live advertising and analytics configuration, including any required certified consent-management platform, is correctly implemented before relying on consent for those regions.

Google provides information about its advertising technologies and controls at Google Advertising Policies and Technologies and Google Privacy Policy.

8. International data transfers

Our service providers may store or process information in countries other than the country where you are located, including outside Uganda or the European Economic Area. Where GDPR transfer rules apply, appropriate safeguards must be used for transfers that require them. You may contact us for further information about relevant safeguards.

9. How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected. Published listing information may remain available while the listing is active and may be retained for a reasonable period after removal for moderation, fraud prevention, dispute handling, backups or legal compliance. Support correspondence and technical records may be retained for as long as needed to resolve the matter, maintain security or meet legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the circumstances.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information. No website, internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your account credentials private, use trusted devices and avoid sending sensitive information through public listings or ordinary email.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and the law that applies, you may have the right to:

  • request access to personal information we hold about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • request deletion of information, subject to lawful exceptions;
  • request restriction of, or object to, certain processing;
  • receive certain information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • complain to a relevant data protection supervisory authority.

To exercise a right, email msngzclntn@gmail.com with “Privacy Request” in the subject line. Tell us what you are requesting and which listing, form or interaction is involved. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where the law permits or requires it. Where GDPR applies, we aim to respond within the time limits required by that law.

12. Children

Layisi is not intended for children to submit personal information independently. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate steps.

13. Third-party websites and links

Listings and pages may link to external websites, application forms, messaging services or other third-party services. Those services are not controlled by Layisi and have their own privacy practices. Review their policies before providing information or completing a transaction.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website, services, legal requirements or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Please review it periodically.

15. Contact

For questions or requests about this policy or your personal information, contact Layisi at msngzclntn@gmail.com.

This policy is intended to explain the website’s current features and provide privacy information. It is not a certification of GDPR compliance or legal advice. Actual compliance depends on the website’s technical configuration, operational practices, contracts with service providers and the laws applicable to its activities.
